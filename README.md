## Game instructions
Race your opponents to the end of the island. When it is your turn press *space* to roll the die, you will move a number of spaces equal to the number you roll on the die. If you land on the same space as an opponent both of you will engage in a rock paper scissors battle against each other. Best of 3 games, loser is knocked back 3 spaces. While in battle *click* on the buttons above your player to make your selection and see if you beat your opponent. The other character on the board with you will also battle each other. Watch their battle in real time to see who will make it to the end and escape the island. Press *S* to save your game and *L* to load the game from the most recent save.

## Strategy Guide
While Janken Island is a chance based game there are a few strategies you can use. The most important thing to note is that each character does not use rock paper and scissors equally, they each have a favorite they like to use. (Rock man) prefers rock (X%) of the time, (Scissors man) prefers scissors (X%) of the time, (Paper guy) prefers paper (X%) of the time. Remember this whenever you face off against each character.

## Cheat Codes:

### IN BOARD:
Ctrl [1,2,3,4]: Increase the respective player’s position on the board by 1 tile.

Alt [1,2,3,4]: Decrease the respective player’s position on the board by 1 tile.

S: Saves game state.

L: Load a saved game state.

Alt+O: Skip to next player’s turn.

ALT+T: Turns on/off turns.

Alt+U: Toggles tile overlap check(the check to see if two players share the same tile).

Alt+E: Restarts the room.

Alt+U: Restarts the game.


### IN RPS BATTLE:
-Ctrl up+ctrl down increase/decrease player 1's health.

-Alt up+alt down increase/decrease player 2's health/

-Ctrl left+Ctrl Right decrease/increase AI's lowest response time.

-Alt left+Alt Right decrease/increase AI's highest response time.
